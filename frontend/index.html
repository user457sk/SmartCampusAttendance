About SmartCampus

SmartCampus is a Student Attendance Management System designed for colleges and schools. Teachers log in and manage attendance only for the subjects assigned to them, while students get transparent, real-time visibility into their own attendance. The system is built on a beginner-friendly Flask backend with a relational database and a modern, responsive HTML/CSS/JavaScript frontend.
